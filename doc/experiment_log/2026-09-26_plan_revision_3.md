# 2026-09-26 — Plan revision 3: survival treated as a caveat on the premise

**Goal:** Record a decision. No new runs were made.

## Context

The M13 entry concluded that the clump-survival estimate (F1) "questions the premise of Experiments A–E for the planetary-mass case". `RESULTS.md` accordingly proposed promoting Experiment D (formation history) ahead of the CMB likelihood work (M5, M7, M8).

## Decision (with the user)

- **The survival estimate is a caveat on the premise, in the same category as Big Bang nucleosynthesis.** Both are already excluded from the scope of the experiments (plan §12). The project's question is conditional: *if* clumps exist throughout the relevant epochs, which properties leave the observations unchanged? The survival result doesn't alter the answer to that question.
- **The results constrain different properties of the same clump.** The drag-based bounds (CMB, small scales) constrain only the surface density Σ; the clump mass drops out of the linear dynamics. The survival criterion constrains mass and internal density at a given epoch. Together they give a fuller description of which clumps would be allowed.
- **What "exist" means.**
  - The CMB results need clumps throughout the acoustic epoch (z ≲ 10⁵ to ≈ 1100), because extra baryons that were diffuse before recombination would load the photon–baryon fluid.
  - The small-scale bound (M12) needs them by z ~ 10⁷ and is reported under that stronger premise.
- **Execution order returns to M5 → M7 → M8 → M9 (C) → M10 → M11.** Formation history, survival and BBN are assessed at M11.
